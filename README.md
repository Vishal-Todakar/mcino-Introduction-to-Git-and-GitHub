## Simple Interest Calculator

A simple interest calculator that calculates interest based on the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period in years.

### Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

### Input

- `p`: Principal amount
- `r`: Annual rate of interest (percentage)
- `t`: Time period in years

### Output

- Simple interest calculated using the formula above.

### Example

If principal = 1000, rate = 5%, and time = 2 years:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100

### Usage

Enter the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period when prompted by the calculator.
